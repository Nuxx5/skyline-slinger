Skyline Slinger

Sunrise over Harbour Ward. Fall, fire a silk line into the nearest tower, and let the pendulum do the rest. The city never ends — it builds itself ahead of you as you swing. Beacons blink above roughly every fourth tower — and patrol drones guard the skyline (or pick free swing and have the city to yourself). Hit a drone at speed, or swing straight through it, to take it down; get shot too often and you wake up on the nearest roof.

  1. Open this page in Safari (not inside another app).
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with the arrow at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Launch Skyline Slinger from your home screen — it opens full-screen like an app, no browser bars.

Works offline once cached by Safari. On Android, Chrome offers "Add to Home screen" from its menu the same way.
