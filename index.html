Skyline Slinger

Fall, fire a silk line into the nearest tower, and let the pendulum do the rest. Seventy beacons are blinking above Harbour Ward. Collect them without touching the street.

  1. Open this page in Safari (not inside another app).
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with the arrow at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Launch Skyline Slinger from your home screen — it opens full-screen like an app, no browser bars.

Works offline once cached by Safari. On Android, Chrome offers "Add to Home screen" from its menu the same way.
